<p>This project contains the custom PCB design for the Smart Water Heater Test Station developed at the PSU Power Engineering Lab. The board integrates sensor signal conditioning, 4–20 mA temperature and flow inputs, MAX1238 ADC data acquisition, Raspberry Pi GPIO interface, relay-based solenoid valve control, RS-485/CTA-2045 communication support, and ACS37800 AC power monitoring.</p>
<p>The PCB is used as the per-unit embedded hardware platform for residential electric water heater testing. Each water heater unit uses one custom PCB with a Raspberry Pi controller to monitor temperature, flow rate, AC voltage, AC current, power, and energy behavior while supporting automated water draw control through a relay-actuated valve.</p>
